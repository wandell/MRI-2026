## Brain data behind the interactive figures

- **Used in:** `chapters/interactive/data-t1map.js` (fitted $T_1$ map),
  `utility/data-vfa.js` (the variable-flip-angle volume it was fitted from)
- **Origin:** the qMRLab mOOC's variable-flip-angle dataset

`data-t1map.js` is generated by `utility/precompute-t1map.js`, which runs the
qMRust `fit_volume` offline so the page loads a 47 kB fitted map rather than a
277 kB raw volume. The map is recalibrated onto literature $T_1$ values in
`chapters/interactive/ir-shared.js`; that is a pedagogical adjustment, not the
raw fit, and the reasoning is documented there.

The two synthetic periventricular lesions in Figure 10.12 are not in the data.
They are placed by `chapters/includes/fig-flair-protocol.qmd`, following the
convention of the mOOC's T1 class application example: $T_1$ raised 10% and
$T_2$ doubled over the white matter they replace.
